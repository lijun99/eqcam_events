About these surveys

Research prototype. These pages are part of a research project on using public camera video to observe the effects of earthquakes. The findings are produced automatically by software and a vision-language model and are not verified. They may be wrong or incomplete. This is not an official earthquake product and must not be used for emergency, safety or other decisions. For official information, follow the U.S. Geological Survey, ShakeAlert, and your local emergency management agency.

What is shown

For each event, a map of the cameras near the epicenter, colored by what the automated review found, and per camera a tile of four low-resolution stills (before, during and after the shaking), a plot of the image motion, and a table of the measurements and findings. The maps link to video clips hosted elsewhere; those clips are not part of this site.

Sources and copyright

The stills come from camera streams that were publicly accessible at the time of the event. All rights to the images remain with their owners. They are reproduced here in small number and at low resolution, without audio, for non-commercial research and to document the effects of earthquakes. No images or findings on this site may be taken as permission to reuse the original camera content.

If you own or operate a camera shown here and want its images credited differently or removed, please tell us (see below); we will act promptly.

Privacy

  • People in the stills are blurred automatically, from boxes placed by the same review that looks for damage. The review can miss a person, especially a small or partly hidden one.
  • We do not try to identify anyone. The findings describe people only as counts and actions (for example, "people gathering").
  • Full-resolution frames and the original stills are not published.

Removal requests and corrections

To ask for an image to be blurred further or removed, for a camera to be taken off, or to report an error, open an issue at github.com/lijun99/eqcam_events/issues. Please do not post a personal image or personal details in the issue: the camera, the event and the time are enough. We remove images first and review afterwards.
